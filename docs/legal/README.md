# Legal and Compliance Documents

Legal material is split between long-form research, editable agreement templates, and generated packets.

## Contents

| Folder | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [research](research/README.md) | Long-form legal and regulatory research. |
| [templates](templates/README.md) | Editable legal templates and customer agreement packets. |
| [dist](dist/) | Generated combined service agreement packets in Markdown, HTML, and PDF formats. |

## Working Notes

- Update the source files in `templates/service-agreements/` before regenerating the packet.
- Run `npm run docs:legal` from the repo root to rebuild the legal packet.
- Keep attorney-review disclaimers in legal documents unless counsel replaces them.
