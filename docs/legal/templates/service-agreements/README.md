# DentalAI Service Agreement Packet

**Status:** Draft templates for attorney review. These documents are not legal advice and are not ready for signature until reviewed by qualified healthcare/regulatory counsel in the states where the product will be used.

This packet is designed for an AI-enabled dental office operations product that may connect to Open Dental, process PHI, generate draft clinical notes, support billing/insurance workflows, send patient communications, and run locally or in a hosted/hybrid environment.

## Recommended Execution Packet

For a first real office deployment, use these documents together:

1. `01-master-services-agreement.md` - primary commercial contract with the dental office.
2. `02-business-associate-agreement.md` - HIPAA business associate agreement.
3. `03-implementation-statement-of-work.md` - install, setup, training, and go-live scope.
4. `04-security-and-data-protection-addendum.md` - operational security commitments and customer responsibilities.
5. `05-open-dental-integration-authorization.md` - authorization to configure and use Open Dental API access.
6. `06-ai-clinical-use-addendum.md` - AI output limitations, provider review, and clinical responsibility.
7. `07-patient-recording-ai-consent.md` - patient-facing consent template if recording/transcription is enabled.
8. `08-staff-recording-ai-policy.md` - office staff policy/acknowledgment template.
9. `09-patient-communications-addendum.md` - SMS/email/recall/nurture messaging terms.
10. `10-billing-insurance-addendum.md` - claims, eligibility, estimates, and billing workflow terms.
11. `11-subprocessor-and-vendor-checklist.md` - internal checklist for BAAs, subprocessors, and enabled vendors.

## Use Notes

- Replace all bracketed placeholders before use.
- Attach the applicable feature addenda only for enabled modules.
- Do not enable audio recording, SMS/email outreach, cloud AI, insurance transactions, payment processing, or remote support until the related agreement/addendum and vendor review are complete.
- If PHI is handled by any vendor, confirm whether a Business Associate Agreement or equivalent written safeguard is required before data is shared.
- For Open Dental API access, keep permissions to the minimum necessary resources and methods.

## Attorney Review Checklist

- HIPAA Privacy, Security, and Breach Notification Rules.
- State dental board recordkeeping rules.
- State recording/wiretapping consent laws.
- TCPA, CAN-SPAM, and state mini-TCPA rules for communications.
- Insurance, claims, coding, and anti-fraud requirements.
- State privacy laws and biometric/voiceprint rules if applicable.
- FDA/clinical decision support posture if radiograph analysis or diagnostic recommendations are enabled.
- Enforceability of warranty disclaimers, liability caps, indemnities, and arbitration/venue clauses.
