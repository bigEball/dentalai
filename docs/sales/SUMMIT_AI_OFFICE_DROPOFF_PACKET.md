# Summit AI Services Office Leave-Behind

## Page 1: What Summit Is

Summit AI Services is an AI operations layer for dental practices.

It helps the team organize the daily work around patient care: notes, front desk tasks, recall, scheduling, treatment follow-up, claims, pre-auths, billing, reports, and staff workflow questions.

Core message:

> Summit helps dental teams see what needs attention, draft the repetitive work, and keep follow-up from falling through the cracks.

What it is not:

- Not a replacement for the dentist.
- Not a replacement for Open Dental.
- Not autonomous clinical decision-making.
- Not a claim payment guarantee.
- Not a substitute for the practice's compliance program.

The practice stays in control. Summit supports the work.

## Page 4: Trust, Compliance, And Next Step

Summit is built for responsible, human-reviewed dental workflows.

Legal and compliance position:

> Summit is built for legal and compliant dental workflow implementation when configured with the proper practice policies, security controls, BAA process, consent workflows, access controls, audit logging, and human review.

What that means:

- Providers make final clinical decisions.
- Providers review and approve notes.
- Billing teams review claims before submission.
- Open Dental remains the system of record when used by the practice.
- Recording or transcription workflows should follow applicable consent policies.
- Security, BAA, access, audit, and retention requirements should be reviewed during implementation.
